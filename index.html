Theoretical Soft, Condensed, and Biological Matter Physics

Our research sits at the interface of soft-matter physics and biological physics. In soft materials, our objectives are application-focused and aimed at addressing industrial challenges such as controlling surface migration of small molecules in complex formulations, predicting properties of confined soft matter, and quantitatively modeling corrosion protection in polymerized coatings.

Soft materials

Surface migration in complex formulations, confinement-driven behavior, and predictive models that connect microstructure to macroscopic performance.

Biological physics

Cell wall mechanics and antimicrobial resistance, nuclear chromosome organization and epigenetic regulation, and biological phase separation in membraneless organelles.

Collaboration

As theoreticians, we collaborate extensively with experimentalists across academia and industry to build quantitative models that can be tested directly.
