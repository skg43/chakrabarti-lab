Theoretical Soft, Condensed, and Biological Matter Physics

Our research sits at the interface of soft-matter physics and biological physics, combining theory and computation with close experimental collaboration across academia and industry.

Soft materials

Application-focused modeling of complex formulations, confinement effects, and quantitative descriptions of corrosion protection in polymerized coatings.

Biological physics

Curiosity-driven work toward clinical relevance: bacterial cell wall mechanics, chromosome organization and epigenetic regulation, and biological phase separation.

Collaboration

We work closely with experimentalists and partners to build predictive models that connect mechanisms to measurable observables.
